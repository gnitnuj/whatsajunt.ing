A little backstory

I grew up in Kawakawa Bay, New Zealand, listening to Enya and dreaming of being a fighter pilot. Later I wanted to figure out the answer to life, the universe and everything. I once told my primary 3 teacher that I'd found a new way to do multiplication. She said, "No, you can't do it that way," so I walked out of class never to return. I've been questioning things ever since.

I loved my SuperNES and rolled deep on a WINDOWS powered PC, then an iMac, blazin' hazin' the WWWs via 56k.

What I'm up to

I run Julu & Co., my software consultancy. I'm especially interested in software that helps people create things.

My own little tools usually start with something I need and can't find elsewhere. I'm curious whether they could be useful to a stranger, too: "Can I get someone I don't know to pay $1 for something I built?"

Away from the keyboard, my sister and I are working on a surf retreat in Medewi, Bali. Our plans, along with other places I've owned, are at Far Far Away Holdings.
